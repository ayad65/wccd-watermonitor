Quick:

This page shows what each survey's layer lets this account do, compares what the app expects with what the layers hold, and recomputes every sample's totals from its taxa rows. Nothing is changed.

Totals: stored in the layer vs recomputed by the app

Recomputed values use the WCCD HILS table (99 taxa). Index = round(count × HILS). Avg HILS = total index ÷ total count. EPT taxa = taxa present in Mayflies, Stoneflies and Caddisflies (taxa 26–39, 49–57, 70–86).

About this build

This is an early, read-only build. It signs in, reads the three survey layers (Pre-construction, Post-construction, Lab analysis) and shows each study area's progress on the Home page. Nothing can be changed from this build.

Data entry, Explore and Reports are added in later builds. The greyed menu items are placeholders.

To report a problem or ask a question, email the administrator at yayad@pennwest.edu. Include the study area ID, what you clicked, and the message on the screen.

This part of the app comes in a later build. The layout is the one you approved in the layout review.
